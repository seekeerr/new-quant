## Key Findings

### 1. Factor Alpha is Real and Large
- **All 8 configs deliver >19% CAGR** — even after full transaction costs
- Best config (5 stocks/quarterly): **22.2% gross → 21.4% net**
- vs FD at 7% → that's **14-15% excess return**
- vs the original strategy at 4% → **the factor was being murdered by the strategy logic**

### 2. Cost Impact is Small (~1-1.6% CAGR)
| Config | Gross CAGR | Net CAGR | Cost Drag |
|--------|-----------|---------|-----------|
| 5 stocks / monthly | 20.6% | 19.0% | **1.6%** |
| 5 stocks / quarterly | 22.2% | 21.4% | **0.8%** |
| 10 stocks / monthly | 21.3% | 19.8% | **1.5%** |
| 10 stocks / quarterly | 20.3% | 19.6% | **0.7%** |

Quarterly rebalancing cuts cost drag in half (0.7-0.8% vs 1.5-1.6%). With ₹5L capital, transaction costs are manageable.

### 3. Monthly vs Quarterly Rebalancing
- **Monthly** gives slightly better **Sharpe** (0.43-0.49 vs 0.30-0.36) and lower **Max DD** (-55% to -59% vs -65% to -68%)
- **Quarterly** gives slightly higher **CAGR** (especially for 5 stocks) but with more volatility
- Monthly rebalancing provides better risk-adjusted returns

### 4. 5 vs 10 Stocks
- Performance is very similar (~20% CAGR for both)
- 10 stocks has slightly better Sharpe in monthly rebalancing
- 5 stocks/quarterly has the highest raw CAGR (22.2%)

### 5. Win Rate is Excellent
- **67-73% win rate** across all configs
- **Profit factor 2.6-2.8x** — winners are meaningfully larger than losers

### 6. But Drawdowns are Savage
- **Max drawdown: -55% to -68%** — this is the price of concentrated momentum
- COVID crash (2020) and 2022 correction visible in all equity curves
- Rolling 3Y CAGR drops to ~-5% during worst periods

## What Killed the Original Strategy (4% CAGR)?

Now we know the factor delivers **~20% CAGR**. So where did the other **16%** go?

| Component | Estimated CAGR Drag |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Trailing stop (2.5× ATR) — stopped out winners | ~3-5% |
| Drawdown liquidation (-15%/-25%) — panic sells | ~3-5% |
| Daily loss halt (-3%, 5 days) — missed recoveries | ~2-3% |
| Strategy blending (diluted momentum with 3 other strategies) | ~2-3% |
| BEAR regime 30% allocation (kept bleeding) | ~1-2% |
| NaN→50 fill in ranker (diluted strong signals) | ~1-2% |
| **Total estimated drag** | **~12-20%** |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The risk management system designed to "protect" capital was actually **the primary source of underperformance**. The cure was worse than the disease.
