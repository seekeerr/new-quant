Step 2 — Asli sawal

Kya wo 0.27% spike ki wajah se tha?

Yeh study ka sabse zaroori control hai, aur isi se poora natija badalta hai.

Socho class mein exam hua aur aapka beta 72 marks laya. Achha hai ya bura? Pata nahi — jab tak class ka average na maloom ho. Agar class average 80 hai, to 72 kharab hai.

Market mein bhi wahi. 2022–2026 India mein bull market tha — har stock upar jaa raha tha. Toh sawal yeh nahi ki spike wale stock ne kitna diya, sawal yeh hai ki us hi din baaki sabne kitna diya.

Jargon translate

Isi ko report mein "excess return" ya "date-demeaned" kaha gaya hai. Simple matlab: us din market ne jo diya wo minus kar do — jo bacha, wahi signal ki asli kamai hai. Yahan wo bachta hai −0.15%.

Step 3 — Poora curve

Sirf 10–12% nahi — har size ka move check kiya

Aur jo pattern nikla wo 10–12% wale sawal se kahin bada hai.

Asli finding

Short-horizon returns India mein mean-revert karte hain, continue nahi. Jo gira hai wo sambhalta hai (−0.39% median, 45.8% win — poore curve mein best), jo bhaaga hai wo thakta hai, aur jo 30%+ bhaag gaya hai use sabse zyada saza milti hai (−2.16% median).

Aur yeh aapke apne system ko validate karta hai: 12-1 momentum jaanbujh ke last 1 mahina skip karta hai — theek isi reversal se bachne ke liye. Yeh study us design choice ka direct proof hai.

Step 4 — Bachane ki koshish

Chaar tarike se signal ko bachane ki koshish ki

Har baar yeh soch ke ki shayad kisi khaas situation mein kaam karta ho. Koi kaam nahi aaya.

Zyada der rok ke rakho?

1, 3, 5, 10 aur 20 din tak hold karke dekha. Raw return badhta gaya (20 din mein +1.46%) — lekin wo poora market drift hai. Excess wahin ka wahin, aur median lagatar gehra hota gaya: −0.35% → −1.64%.

Fail

Sirf bade, liquid stocks mein?

₹1 cr, ₹5 cr aur ₹25 cr ADV — teen liquidity tiers. Jitna liquid, utna kam bura (−0.19% → −0.08%), lekin kabhi positive nahi. Chhote/illiquid names mein effect zyada bura hai — yeh noise-driven pops ka classic signature hai.

Fail

Kya kisi ek saal ki galti hai?

5 mein se 4 saal excess mean negative, aur median paanchon saal negative. 2023 mein halka positive mean (+0.19%) aaya lekin median wahan bhi −0.57% tha. Yeh regime artifact nahi, stable pattern hai.

Fail

Sirf uptrend wale stocks mein?

200-DMA ke upar + positive 12-1 momentum wale stocks — sabse achha case. Wahan bhi excess mean sirf +0.06%, median abhi bhi −0.73%, win rate 43.8% (baseline 45%). Best case = break-even, aur wo bhi cost se pehle.

Step 5 — Cost ki deewar

Best case bhi cost se 8 guna peeche hai

5-din ke round trip par ₹1 lakh position ka kharcha 0.49% hai. Har variant ko us hurdle ke saamne rakh ke dekhiye.

Iska matlab yeh nahi ki signal "thoda kamzor" hai. Iska matlab yeh hai ki signal ki theoretical best kamai bhi kharche ka pachchisva hissa hai. Cost kam karke bhi yeh theek nahi hota — discount broker par bhi STT aur slippage bache rahenge.

Recommendations

Toh iska karna kya hai

Spike dekh ke mat kudiye

"Yeh to 11% bhaag gaya, aur bhaagega" — yeh soch 16,508 cases par galat sabit hoti hai. Cost se pehle halka negative, cost ke baad pakka nuksan. Is par koi strategy mat banaiye.

Entry-timing rule — yeh actually kaam ka hai

Jab aapki momentum strategy kisi stock ko "buy" bolti hai aur wo stock abhi-abhi 5 din mein 10%+ bhaag chuka hai, to ek hafta ruk jaaiye. Data kehta hai rukne se kuch nahi khoya, aur typical case mein ~0.87% sasta entry mila. Yeh nayi strategy nahi — purani strategy ka sasta entry hai.

Downtrend ka pop = avoid filter

200-DMA ke neeche wale stock mein 10–12% ka pop 5 din mein 0.55% underperform karta hai, sirf 39.8% win rate ke saath. Standalone short strategy ke liye yeh bhi cost hurdle se chhota hai — lekin ek avoid list ke roop mein legitimate hai.

Bucket edges tune mat kijiye

5% se 30% tak ka poora range ek hi kahani keh raha hai. Yeh 10–12% cut-point ki property nahi, India mein short-horizon reversal ki property hai. Yahan "jo bucket kaam karta hai" dhoondhna wahi overfitting hogi jo is project mein pehle se banned hai.

Method & caveats

Yeh numbers kaise nikle

Design

  • Survivorship-free. Universe NSE daily bhavcopy se bana hai, jismein delisted aur merged names bhi hain — sirf aaj ke survivors nahi. Isi wajah se yeh project ke purane 47.7% CAGR jaise inflated numbers se bachta hai.
  • ETF leakage band. NSE bhavcopy mein ETFs bhi SERIES=="EQ" ke naam se aate hain. Sirf ISIN INE (asli equity) rakhe gaye — 3,291 names.
  • Look-ahead nahi. Signal close[t] par dikhta hai aur entry bhi close[t] par hai. Trend context (200-DMA, 12-1 momentum) sab strictly backward-looking.
  • Overlap handle kiya. Ek stock lagatar 5 din trigger kar sakta hai, isse sample artificially bada lagta hai. Isliye ek non-overlapping variant bhi chalaya (per stock 7+ din ka gap, n=12,084) aur har date ko ek observation maana — mean daily excess −0.303%, 1,034 dates, t = −2.89.
  • Filters. t−5 se t+5 tak har session trade hua ho; close ≥ ₹10; median 20-day turnover ≥ ₹5 cr (primary tier).

Caveats — jo is study ki limits hain

  • Prices bhavcopy par gap-detection se back-adjust hue hain (Upstox ke against median single-day error ~0.25%). Kuch unadjusted corporate actions spike buckets mein baith sakte hain — 30%+ bucket mein yeh known noise source hai.
  • Entry close[t] par maana gaya hai, cost model se zyada slippage nahi. Asli mein bhaagte hue stock ka fill kharab milta hai — yaani asli result isse bhi bura hoga.
  • Excess equal-weighted cross-section ke against hai, sector-neutral ya beta-adjusted nahi. Spike bucket mein high-beta names zyada hain, isliye 25% extra volatility ka ek hissa beta hai, pure idiosyncratic risk nahi.
  • Main tables (buckets, years, liquidity, context) overlapping events par hain — unka n effective sample se bada hai. Significance ke liye sirf non-overlapping t-stat dekhein.
