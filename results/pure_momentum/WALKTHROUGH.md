## Key takeaway

All four configs grew Rs 5L into the Rs 28L-82L range but carry brutal max drawdowns (-56% to -68%), and **all peaked in mid-to-late 2024 and remain in their deepest-ever drawdown at the backtest end (May 2026)** — none had recovered the prior peak. This is the risk that motivated the market-filter experiment (see `../market_filters/WALKTHROUGH.md`).
